## 2. SEO / content observations

1. **Two separate IndiaMART seller accounts** for the same GSTIN/CIN, with different contact persons, different phone routes, different names ("Private Limited" vs "Pvt. Ltd.") and split catalogues (machines vs services). This splits lead flow, dilutes ranking and looks inconsistent to buyers. [IM1] [IP1]
2. **Zero product content:** 10 IndiaMART items with no photos, specs, prices, MOQ or descriptions; no product-detail pages are generated (PDP flag 0). IndiaMART's search ranking and "Get Best Price" lead routing strongly favour priced, spec-rich listings — competitors show prices (₹11 L – ₹2 Cr) and specs. [IM3] [IMB]
3. **Generic stock imagery** — category thumbnails are IndiaMART admin images; the only company photo is a near-blank wall. [IM7]
4. **Catalogue gaps vs actual capability:** services (layouts, P&IDs, hydraulics, erection supervision) are on a different account; no spares/retrofit items; no "CCM upgrade", "tundish", "mould oscillator", "withdrawal straightening unit", "hot-top casting table" keywords — all high-intent search terms. [IM1] [IP1] **[INFERENCE]**
5. **Typos / quality:** "in for improvement", "belive", "metel handling", "engineering solution's". [IM1] [IM7] [TI2]
6. **Business-type inconsistency:** Manufacturer (IndiaMART 1) vs Service Provider (IndiaMART 2, TradeIndia) vs MCA NIC "business activities n.e.c." [IM2] [IP2] [TI1] [ZC1]
7. **Address inconsistency:** MCA registered office vs TradeIndia address (both Kondhwa, different premises); no works address anywhere. [ZC1] [TI1]
8. **Website is a placeholder** (no indexable content, no contact data, no sitemap pages). Brand spelled three ways: Mscast / MsCast / MScast; e-mail domain "mcast.co.in" differs from website domain "mscastengineering.com". [WEB1] [IB1] [LI2]
9. **No proof content:** no references, installation list, case studies, testimonials, videos, brochures, certifications (ISO), or trade-fair presence. [IM2] [SRCH]
10. **Brand-name collision** with IGNOU "MSCAST" and Malta "MCAST" makes branded search noisy — use "MSCAST Engineering" + "continuous casting" consistently. [SRCH]
