## 3. Industry context

### 3.1 Indian secondary steel (CCM market driver)
- India produced ~168–169 Mt crude steel in FY2025-26; capacity ~220 Mtpa; National Steel Policy target 300 Mt by 2030-31. [LOH]
- Secondary sector (IF/EAF/small DRI-based) contributes ~45–50% of production; "Induction furnace units dominate India's billet and ingot production"; EAF expected to grow with CBAM / green-steel demand. [LOH] **[secondary blog source — treat figures as indicative]**
- Scrap consumption ~35–41 Mt in FY2025, projected 70–80 Mt by 2030 as IF/EAF capacity expands. [LOH]
- Electrotherm alone reports >6,500 induction melting furnaces supplied to the steel industry and turnkey mini steel plants up to 1 Mtpa [ELT] — indicates a very large installed base of IF melt shops that need CCMs, retrofits and spares.
- Market price points for CCMs on IndiaMART: ₹75 L – ₹2 Cr per machine (Electramech, Kolkata) [EMI]; refurbished 2-strand Concast 4/7 & 6/11 CCM ₹1.85 Cr (Mexxiss, Ahmedabad) [MEX]; new steel billet casters ₹1.0–1.95 Cr (Fidus Achates, Kruzcon, India Powermelt) [IMB]. Implication: **one CCM order can equal or exceed MSCAST's entire declared annual turnover** → lumpy, project-driven revenue. **[INFERENCE]**

### 3.2 Indian aluminium extrusion & billet casting
- India aluminium-extrusion demand: 795 kt (2025) → ~858 kt (2026), +7.9% y/y; installed extrusion capacity ~3 Mtpa but output only 1.2–1.3 Mt (40–43% utilisation). [ALC] (AlCircle, 1 Aug 2026)
- Building & construction ~280–300 kt of extrusion demand, growing 8–10%/yr; ~50% of India's ~4.2 Mt aluminium comes via the secondary (scrap) route; heavy dependence on imported scrap (~30% from Middle East). [ALC]
- Low utilisation suggests **price pressure on new-capacity capex** but steady demand for **billet-quality upgrades / in-house billet casting** to cut billet cost. **[INFERENCE]**
- IndiaMART aluminium billet casting machine price points: ₹11.5 L – ₹20 L (Mettherm, Manor Hydraulics, Max Hydro Pneumatic, Rajakumar Engineering) [IMB].

### 4.4 Competitive observations **[INFERENCE]**
- Nearly every competitor listing shows **prices, capacities and specs**; MSCAST shows none → MSCAST ranks low and captures few IndiaMART leads.
- Large players sell **turnkey melt shop + CCM + rolling mill**; MSCAST's niche is likely **engineering-heavy custom CCMs, retrofits, layouts & supervision** — value proposition should be made explicit (installed references, radius/strand range, retrofit expertise).
- Aluminium billet casting is a crowded low-price segment (₹11–20 L machines) in India; differentiation must come from automation (drop-speed/water recipes), billet quality, and after-sales.
