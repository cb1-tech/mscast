## 4. Business description (as the company describes itself)

- Listing 1 (products, primary category "Casting, Moulding & Forging Machines"): "We are experts in continuous casting equipments. We provide innovative solutions in for improvement in the casting processes. Also we have a team of engineers who are experts in the conveyors." Quality statement: "we belive in 100% quality check before any despatch of the material." [IM1] [IM7]
- Listing 2 (services, primary category "CAD CAM Design & Consultancy"): "We are committed to provide advanced engineering solutions that add value to industrial processes. Mscast Engineering is committed to provide accurate, high quality services tailored to individual requirements of each project. It is our objective to be fair, time bound & result oriented in all our activities." [IP1] [IP2]
- **Vision:** "We aim to be leading company in all spheres of our activities & provide our customers excellent engineering solution's & services that meet & exceed their expectations." [IP2]
- **Mission:** innovative solutions to improve customers' processes & equipment performance; build own technical competence by developing/acquiring state-of-the-art technology; be a unique engineering-solutions partner; long-term customer relationships; economical & timely engineering solutions. [IP2]
- TradeIndia: "has gained immense expertise in offering Designing Of Casting Machines, Metel [sic] Handling"; business type "Service Provider"; "Registered in 2011". [TI1] [TI2]
- Nature of business per IndiaMART factsheet: listing 1 "Exporter and Manufacturer" (additional: Buyer-Company, Service Provider); listing 2 "Exporter and Service Provider" (additional: Supplier). [IM2] [IP2]
- Own website tagline: "Engineering Excellence Redefined". [WEB1]
